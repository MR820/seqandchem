Molecular Formula & Weight Calculator

Calculate the molecular weight and elemental composition from a chemical formula, or get the formula and weight from a SMILES string.

Supports brackets and multipliers: (NH4)2SO4, Ca(OH)2

How molecular weight is calculated

The calculator sums the IUPAC standard atomic weights of every atom in the formula. This is the average molecular weight — the value used for concentration calculations (e.g. "dissolve 1 mmol in 1 mL"). The monoisotopic mass used in mass spectrometry is slightly different because it uses the most abundant isotope of each element.

Formula syntax

  • C6H12O6 — glucose
  • (NH4)2SO4 — ammonium sulfate, brackets multiplied out
  • Ca(OH)2 — calcium hydroxide
  • Element symbols are case sensitive: Co is cobalt, CO is carbon monoxide

Frequently asked questions

Why does the formula change after conversion?

OpenBabel outputs the formula in Hill notation: carbon first, hydrogen second, then all other elements alphabetically. H8N2O4S and (NH4)2SO4 describe the same compound.

Does it account for charge or isotopes?

No — the calculation treats every atom as its neutral, naturally abundant isotope.
