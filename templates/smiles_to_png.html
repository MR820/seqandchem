SMILES to PNG — molecule image generator

Turn a SMILES string into a raster structure image you can drop into a slide, a report or a database record. The drawing is laid out by OpenBabel and rendered as a 300 × 300 PNG.

PNG or SVG?

  • PNG — fixed resolution, universally supported, ideal for slides and chat.
  • SVG — vector, infinitely scalable, ideal for papers, posters and print. Use the 2D structure generator for SVG output.

Frequently asked questions

Why is the image small?

The default canvas is 300 × 300 pixels. For a larger figure, use the SVG output and scale it, or render at higher resolution with a local OpenBabel install.

Can I get a transparent background?

The PNG output has a white background. SVG output keeps a transparent background.
