Molecular Property Calculator

Calculate molecular weight, formula, logP, TPSA, hydrogen bond donors and acceptors, rotatable bonds, molar refractivity, canonical SMILES, InChI and InChIKey.

Multiple molecules are allowed — one per line or a multi-record SDF.

Descriptors provided

  • Molecular weight (MW) — average mass in g/mol.
  • Formula — molecular formula in Hill notation.
  • logP — octanol/water partition coefficient, a lipophilicity measure.
  • TPSA — topological polar surface area, correlated with membrane permeability.
  • HBD / HBA — hydrogen bond donors and acceptors (Lipinski's rule of five).
  • Rotatable bonds — molecular flexibility indicator.
  • MR — molar refractivity.
  • InChI / InChIKey — standard identifiers for database lookup.

Drug-likeness

These are the descriptors used by Lipinski's rule of five: MW ≤ 500, logP ≤ 5, HBD ≤ 5, HBA ≤ 10, plus TPSA ≤ 140 Å² and rotatable bonds ≤ 10 as common extensions. Use the table above to check whether a compound is likely to be orally active.
